The bundled `mock-ecg-60m.i16le` fixture was generated with NeuroKit2 0.2.13
(MIT):
https://github.com/neuropsychology/NeuroKit

The mock ECG is a synthetic ECGSYN signal (60 minutes at 130 Hz, seed 2709,
72 bpm, noise 0.01). Its normalized values were scaled by 400 and rounded to
signed 16-bit little-endian microvolt samples. This is simulation data, not a
human recording or a clinical signal. NeuroKit2 is used at fixture-generation
time; the packaged app does not require Python or NeuroKit2.

Makowski, D., Pham, T., Lau, Z. J., Brammer, J. C., Lespinasse, F., Pham, H.,
Schölzel, C., & Chen, S. A. (2021). NeuroKit2: A Python toolbox for
neurophysiological signal processing. Behavior Research Methods, 53(4),
1689–1696. https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-020-01516-y
